Tip #1

Ask: "What if AI could help?"

Small experiments often lead to big workflow improvements.

"Curiosity drives innovation."
Ask...
What if AI could approach this problem differently than we would?
What if AI could surface insights hidden in the information?
What if AI could help redesign the workflow itself?

Just ask AI...
Not a developer?

Not a data expert?

Not familiar with a new field?

Facing a complex problem outside your expertise?

Have an idea but don't know where to start?
